Identity & Vision

A DESTINATION WHERE CRICKET PASSION MEETS PERFORMANCE.

Jimmy Sports Club was conceived with a clear vision: to elevate sporting culture in Muzaffarpur and provide cricketers of all skill levels with the quality infrastructure they deserve.

More than just a playing ground, JSC is where young athletes build discipline, competitive players hone their technique, and cricket enthusiasts come together under floodlights. By combining an all-weather artificial turf, covered training nets, automated bowling simulators, and dedicated academy coaching, JSC is empowering the next generation of cricketing talent across Bihar.

Player Development Pathway

JIMMY SPORTS CLUB ACADEMY

Cultivating technical excellence, game intelligence, and competitive spirit in aspiring young cricketers across Muzaffarpur and Bihar.

STRUCTURED CRICKET COACHING & SKILL GROOVING

JSC Academy provides young players with an all-round development environment. Our coaching methodology blends orthodox fundamentals with modern training aids—including automated bowling simulators, video analysis feedback, and regular match simulations on the turf.

Whether learning the first forward defensive stroke or preparing for district and school tournaments, our trainees benefit from dedicated focus, fitness drills, and consistent net practice.

Academy Admission Enquiry

Technical Stance & Grip

Emphasis on balance, head position, backlift, and orthodox footwork against pace and spin.

Bowling Action Mechanics

Biomechanical guidance for fast bowlers and spinners to build rhythm, consistency, and avoid injury.

Machine Repetition

Controlled bowling machine sessions to ingrain muscle memory on cuts, pulls, and drives.

Match Simulation
